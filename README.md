## About me

I'm Naim, from Dhaka, and I'm working towards being a data scientist. The part I like is the whole loop: get a messy dataset, clean it until it stops lying, find out what's actually in it, then decide whether a chart answers the question or a model has to.

So far that has meant exploratory analysis and dashboards (Olympic 2024 results, a bank's loan book, a pizza chain's sales, HR attrition), plus machine learning and pattern recognition labs in Jupyter. Before data I wrote PHP and Laravel, which is why my SQL is better than my statistics. I'm fixing that.

- **Now:** data science projects end to end, from cleaning to a model I can explain
- **Learning:** statistics, ML and deep learning, and model evaluation that holds up outside the notebook
- **Ask me about:** pandas, Power BI, or why your join produced 4× the rows
